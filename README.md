# Nu, Pogodi! - Pixel Platformer

A free, non-commercial, unofficial fan-made pixel platformer inspired by the Soviet cartoon *Nu, Pogodi!* (Ну, погоди!).

**Website:** https://kozlo-pdf.github.io/nu_pogodi/

**Play / download:** visit the [website](https://kozlo-pdf.github.io/nu_pogodi/) or grab [`download/NuPogodi.exe`](https://kozlo-pdf.github.io/nu_pogodi/download/NuPogodi.exe) (Windows 10/11, 64-bit).

- Chapter 1: a chase platformer (3 levels).
- Chapter 2: a classic platformer with 6 boss fights.
- Chapter 3: road-trip platformer with moving platforms, hazards and 6 new bosses.
- Chapter 4: a first-person maze shooter (6 levels, 6 bosses).
- English / Polski / Русский.

## How long to beat
Measured by the author's own playthrough (so a first-time player will probably need longer):

| Stat | Time |
|---|---|
| Full win (all chapters) | 25:14 |
| Average play session | 6:18 |
| One level round | about 18 seconds |

Chapters unlock in order, and progress is saved automatically.
